*Effective November 14, 2026*

Texas Bible Bowl is operated by Bridges to the World, a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization doing
business as Texas Bible Bowl ("we", "us"). This policy covers the website at texasbiblebowl.org,
the study app on that site, and the Texas Bible Bowl app for Android.

In short: we collect what we need to run the event and the study tools, we do not sell it, we show
no ads, and we use no advertising or analytics trackers.

## What we collect

**You can study without an account.** The study materials, quizzes and downloads need no sign-in,
and using them sends us nothing about you beyond the ordinary server logs described below.

**If you create an account**, we store:

- your email address and display name;
- your password, kept only as a one-way hash — we cannot read it;
- your birthdate, or that you are an adult, which decides your competition division;
- contact details you choose to add: mailing address, phone number and how you prefer to be
  contacted;
- the roles your account holds, such as coach or grader.

**If you take part in the event**, your coach, a parent or you give us registration information,
and event staff add to it:

- name, birthdate or school grade, and gender;
- congregation and team;
- T-shirt size;
- for adults, contact details and any volunteer positions;
- cabin and tribe assignments at the event;
- a tester number and the scores you earn in each round.

**If you contribute to the question bank**, we store the questions you submit and the votes you
cast, linked to your account. Questions appear to other users with your display name.

**Server logs.** Like any web service, our servers record requests: the address requested, the
time, and your IP address. We use these to keep the service running and to limit abuse.

**On your device.** The website keeps a few items in your browser's local storage: your sign-in
token so you stay signed in, a copy of the current season's details so pages load quickly, and
your quiz sound preference. The Android app stores your quiz sound preference. We set no
advertising or tracking cookies.

**What we don't collect.** The Android app asks for no device permissions beyond internet access:
no location, contacts, camera, microphone or files. We collect no payment card details — online
donations are handled entirely by PayPal.

## How we use it

- To run the event: registration, team rosters, housing, check-in, scoring and awards.
- To provide your account, and to email you a code when you ask to reset your password.
- To contact coaches and adult participants about the event.
- To show contestants and their parents their own scores once scores are released.
- To keep the service secure.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

## Who can see it

- **Event staff and volunteers** who need it for their job: registrars, graders, housing and
  tribe coordinators, and administrators.
- **Your coach**, for the people registered with your congregation.
- **Other users of the app** see only your display name, on questions you contributed. Scores
  are visible to the contestant, to whoever manages their registration, and to event staff.
- **Service providers** that host the service for us: Fly.io (servers), Neon (database) and our
  email provider (password-reset emails). They process the data on our behalf and may not use it
  for their own purposes.
- **Authorities**, if the law requires it.

## Other services our pages use

Some pages load content from other companies, which then receive your IP address and browser
details under their own privacy policies: Google Fonts and the jsDelivr content network (page
styling), Google Maps (the maps on our Locations page) and PayPal (the Donate page). Event photo
albums are hosted on Google Photos and open on Google's site.

## Children

Texas Bible Bowl is a youth event, so much of the information we hold is about children. Nearly
all of it is given to us by a coach or a parent when a congregation registers.

Children under 13 cannot create an account, and we do not knowingly collect personal information
from them directly. A parent or guardian can create their own account and link it to their child's
registration with the code from the child's coach, then follow the registration and scores for
them. If you are a parent or guardian and want to see, correct or delete what we hold about your child, email us and
we will do so.

## How long we keep it

- **Accounts** are kept until you delete them.
- **Event records** — registrations, rosters and results — are kept as the event's history.
- **Password-reset codes** expire after 15 minutes.
- **Server logs** are kept by our hosting provider for a limited time.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself at any time:

- **On the website:** sign in, open [your account page](/app/#account), and choose **Delete
  account** at the bottom of the page.
- **In the Android app:** open **Account** and choose **Delete account** at the bottom.

You will be asked for your password to confirm. If you cannot sign in, email
[info@texasbiblebowl.org](mailto:info@texasbiblebowl.org) from the account's email address and we
will delete it for you.

**What is deleted:** your sign-in, email address, display name, password, contact details and
roles. People you added to your account who were never registered for an event are deleted too.
This happens immediately and cannot be undone.

**What is kept:**

- Questions you contributed stay in the question bank, shown as written by "Deleted user".
- Anyone who is on an event registration — including you — stays on it as the event's record:
  name, birthdate, congregation, team and results. Your own contact details are removed from it.

To have event records removed as well, email
[info@texasbiblebowl.org](mailto:info@texasbiblebowl.org).

## Your choices

You can see and correct your profile on your account page at any time. For anything else — a copy
of what we hold about you, a correction to a registration, or removal of event records — email us.

## Security

Connections to the site and app are encrypted. Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes.
Access to registration and scoring information is limited to the people whose role requires it.
No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect what you give us.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will post the new version here and update the effective date.
